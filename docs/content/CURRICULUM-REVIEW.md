# Transport curriculum review

30 current entries + 96 proposed additions = 126 planned concepts. **Only 30 are currently reachable.** Proposals have no installed pictures, alt text, sound plans or acceptance. They are not scored or exposed in the learner app. Source: content/transport-extension.psv.

The six undated lessons retain their existing IDs. Levels are proposed word/concept demands, not ages. Five additional collections are proposed alongside the existing ten. Repeated uses across lessons do not add to the concept count. Aliases are not extra entries. Vehicle subtypes are retained only for visible, useful contrasts: kick/motor scooter; pulled/cycle/auto-rickshaw; boat/rowboat/sailboat/canoe/kayak; shuttle/rocket.

New kinds require context-aware runtime adapters. Actions use sequences; parts use whole-subject context; abstract safety concepts stay out of independent picture/Detective tests until a defensible task is authored. No automatic Land/Water/Air membership is proposed for people, places or parts.
