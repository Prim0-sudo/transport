# Transport teaching-art decisions

This is the per-entry continuation of the supplied 60-variant audit, not another full audit or an approval certificate. Miniature World remains the selected direction. Current runtime keeps the pinned original bank while changed/new teaching pictures await review. All 60 original files remain unchanged. Hashes, dimensions, alpha-channel presence and unknown provenance are recorded in TEACHING-ART-LEDGER.json.

Keep means preserve a useful original; repair means change a specified defect while retaining the useful subject; replace requires a clearer factual teaching subject. Keep-alternate preserves an uninstalled candidate. Retarget means the old label was wrong for the picture; it cannot silently replace the current subject. No unknown prompt/license has been invented.
