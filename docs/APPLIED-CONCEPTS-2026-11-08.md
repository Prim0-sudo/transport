# Transport applied-game concept choices

Proposals only, 8 November 2026. No selected concept, detailed preview approval or asset-production authority yet. Concepts use Transport identity, not Plants mechanics. User selection is requested in the chat.

| | Transport Dispatch | Build the Journey | Safe Street |
|---|---|---|---|
| Objective | Match vehicle to purpose, passengers/cargo and travel surface | Understand connected routes, surfaces and obstacles | Recognize a controlled safe crossing sequence with an adult |
| Main action | Choose vehicle, select suitable route and deliver | Connect/replace route pieces then send a suitable vehicle | Stop traffic, inspect clear safe phase, guide group across |
| Visible world | Depot, stops, harbour and rail terminal on a transport map | Route board with road/rail/water links and destinations | Street scene with stop line, crossing, pavement and waiting group |
| Correctable mistake | Unsuitable vehicle/surface stays at depot with explanation; change choice | Bad link remains highlighted; remove/replace before retry | Unsafe action leaves group waiting; show the missing safety step |
| Completion | Finish a small set of distinct useful deliveries | Complete and traverse the connected journey | Complete several authored crossings without duplicating rewards |
| Difficulty | More destinations/purpose constraints, independently chosen round length | More junctions/obstacles; bounded reachable layouts | More visual contexts and relevant decisions, not faster pressure to cross |
| Art/contact challenge | Vehicle orientation, road/rail/water support and stop alignment | Connected route geometry, bridge/track contacts and vehicle turns | Feet on pavement/crossing, clear vehicle stops and adult accompaniment |

All need tap/select-place and keyboard alternatives, gentle feedback, clear replay/reset, responsive phone composition, hidden/Teacher holds, reduced-motion equivalents and owned cleanup. New coordinated movement uses GSAP and MotionPath for curves. A rebuilt environment requires four genuine registered planes plus independent actors/UI.
